Caesar Cipher Kya Hai?

Classic Substitution

Yeh tareeqa Julius Caesar ne apne khufiya paighamat bhejne ke liye istemal kiya tha. Is mein har letter (A-Z) ko aik khas number (Shift) ke mutabiq aage ya peeche khiskaya jata hai. Maslan agar Shift 3 ho to 'A' ban jata hai 'D'.

Istemal: Yeh aaj kal sirf seekhne ke liye istemal hota hai kyunke sirf 25 shifts check karke isay aasani se tora ja sakta hai.
